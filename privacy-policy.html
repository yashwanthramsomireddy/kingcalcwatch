Privacy Policy — King Calc Watch

Last updated: 16 October 2026

King Calc Watch is a standalone calculator, currency converter and unit converter for Wear OS, published by TeamExyKings. This policy covers King Calc Watch specifically.

Data collection

King Calc Watch does not collect, store on any server, or transmit any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

What is stored on your watch

Your choices (theme, accent color, key style, Compact/Expanded calculator mode, the keep-screen-on option, your selected and favourite currencies, and your selected unit category and units), your recent calculation history, and the most recently downloaded exchange rates are saved locally on the watch so the app remembers them. This data never leaves the device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Network access

The calculator and unit converter work entirely offline. The currency converter is the only feature that uses the internet: it downloads public exchange rates from the ExchangeRate-API open service (open.er-api.com). These requests contain only the requested base currency — no personal information, device identifiers, or usage data are sent by the app. As with any internet request, the service provider can see the network address the request comes from; see the exchangerate-api.com terms and privacy policy for how they handle that. If the watch is offline, the converter keeps working with the last saved rates.

Permissions

King Calc Watch requests the Internet permission, used only by the currency converter as described above. It requests no access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera, health data, or any other personal information.

Children's privacy

Since the app collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted here with a revised date.
